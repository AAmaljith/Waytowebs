Waytowebs · Interview concept

Product wireframes

Nineteen core screens across customer, dealer, plumber, and admin journeys. These are experience concepts for discussion, not final visual design.

Prototype boundary OTP verification (with demo console delivery), Zoho dealer verification, and role-filtered product data are proven in the demo. Customer profile persistence, cart, payment, credit management, push notifications, plumber activities, and admin screens are proposed product flows.
