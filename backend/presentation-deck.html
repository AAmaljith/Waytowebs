02 · Context

One catalogue. Four role-aware experiences.

The product connects a trade-supply business to the people who buy, resell, and install its products.

Customers01

Browse products, see retail price and availability, pay upfront, and track an order.

Dealers02

Zoho-verified access, dealer pricing, exact stock, and admin-enabled payment terms.

Plumbers03

Admin-approved sign-in, assigned activities, schedules, and service notifications.

Admins04

Manage people, products, orders, payments, dealer credit, and reports.

Product principle: make role-specific data decisions on the server. Hiding a field in the app is not access control.

10 · Checkout and credit

Payment verified by webhook. Credit checked fail-closed.

Customer · upfrontProposed
1 · CheckoutServer rechecks item price, availability, GST and delivery.
↓
2 · PayGateway handles payment; signed webhook confirms it to backend.
↓
3 · FulfilIdempotent Zoho order job; retry if Zoho is temporarily down.
Dealer · upfront / termsProposed
1 · Select termPay now, or admin-enabled 30/90-day credit.
↓
2 · Check policyAdmin-set limit + term; Zoho-synced outstanding + overdue.
↓
3 · Create documentoutstanding + order ≤ limit; due date included in Zoho write.
Policy ownership: admin sets credit limits and allowed terms in the app database; Zoho is authoritative for outstanding/overdue balances. If balance data is stale or unavailable, reject credit checkout. Queue and retry failed Zoho document creation.

Credit rejection cases: term not enabled · limit exceeded · overdue invoices (if policy blocks) · Zoho balance unavailable/stale. No credit is approved on uncertainty.

Confirm Zoho document type, due-date fields, and credit policy with the business10 / 12

12 · Assumptions and questions

Decisions to confirm before committing scope.

Zoho product and regionThe brief says “Zoho Workbook,” while the current connector uses Zoho Inventory. Which product, data center, and organization are expected?
Dealer pricingOne shared dealer price list, or different lists/discounts by dealer?
Credit ownershipCan admin-set limits/30- and 90-day terms live in our DB while balances come from Zoho?
Credit rulesAre overdue invoices a hard block? How fresh must balance sync be at checkout?
Plumber verificationRegistration details, ID documents, approval authority, and rejection process?
Plumber job scopeDo plumbers see assigned jobs, buy supplies, or both? Who assigns and updates status?
Payments and messagingPreferred gateway (e.g. Razorpay), SMS provider (e.g. MSG91), and refund flow?
Delivery and taxWho fulfils orders and owns tracking? How are GST and delivery charges calculated?
Working assumptions: customer pays upfront; dealer may pay upfront or use admin-enabled credit; orders sync to Zoho only after verified payment or approved credit. Confirm these with the interviewer.
