1. QUICK START INSTRUCTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Install and launch "app-release.apk" on any Android device (Android 8.0+).
2. On the first screen, choose the user role you wish to test.
3. Enter the corresponding test phone number listed below.
4. Tap "Send OTP".
5. Enter the 6-digit verification code: 123456
6. Tap "Verify OTP" to enter the role-specific dashboard.

*NOTE ON BACKEND COLD START:
The backend is deployed on a free cloud instance on Render. If it has been idle,
the very first request on app launch may take 20-30 seconds to wake up the server.
Subsequent requests will be immediate.

[A] DEALER FLOW (Wholesale & Credit Access)
-------------------------------------------
Mobile Number : 9895673607   (Alternative: 8281292173)
OTP           : 123456
Role to Select: Dealer

What this demonstrates:
- Zoho Verification Gatekeeper: The backend queries Zoho Inventory contacts
  in real-time to verify that "Is Dealer = Yes" and "Verified = Yes".
- Live Stock Counts: Unlike the customer view, dealers can see the exact
  available stock quantity on every product card (e.g., "45 in stock") and in the cart.
- Wholesale Pricing: Products reflect approved dealer discount rates.
- Zoho Credit Checkout: Add items to cart -> Checkout -> Select "Credit Payment - 30 Days".

  Sales Order directly in Zoho Inventory with the 30-day payment due date!

*Negative Test to Try: Try logging in as a Dealer with any random unregistered
number (e.g., 9999999999). The app will block it with:
"We could not verify this number for dealer access. Contact your admin."

What this demonstrates:

What this demonstrates:

[D] ADMIN FLOW (Full Business Management Console)
-------------------------------------------------
Mobile Number : 9000000000   (or 9895673607)
OTP           : 123456
Role to Select: Admin

What this demonstrates:
- Overview Metrics: Live business analytics cards (Total Customers, Total Dealers,

- Manage Orders: View all customer/dealer orders, update shipment dates (YYYY-MM-DD),
  and set delivery methods.
- Manage Customers: View and add customer contacts directly into Zoho.
